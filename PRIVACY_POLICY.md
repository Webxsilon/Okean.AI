# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Okean.AI is an open-source AI role-playing application built around user privacy, freedom, and control.

We believe that users should have control over their data and how they interact with AI services.

This Privacy Policy explains how Okean.AI handles information when you use the application.

## 2. Data Collection

Okean.AI does not intentionally collect personal information or transmit user data to its own servers.

The application does not require:
- An account or registration.
- Personal information to use its core features.
- A subscription to access locally available functionality.

Okean.AI does not operate its own user-data collection or analytics service.

## 3. Local Data Storage

Information created or imported within Okean.AI is stored locally on your device.

This may include:
- Conversations and chat history.
- Characters and their descriptions.
- Custom personas.
- Application settings.
- Downloaded AI models.
- Custom background music and other local resources.

Okean.AI does not automatically upload this locally stored information to its own servers.

You are responsible for managing your local files, backups, and device security.

## 4. Third-Party AI Services

Okean.AI supports third-party AI services through API integrations.

When you choose to use an online AI provider, relevant information may be transmitted directly to that provider.

Depending on the service and your configuration, this may include:
- Conversation messages.
- Character and persona information included in prompts.
- Other context required to generate responses.
- API requests and technical metadata.
- Your API key, which is used to authenticate requests.

These requests are handled by the selected third-party provider, not by Okean.AI's own servers.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies, data retention practices, and terms of service. We recommend reviewing them before using their APIs.

## 5. Local AI Engines

Okean.AI supports local AI engines such as Ollama and llama.cpp.

When using a locally running model, AI inference takes place on your device. Conversation content does not need to be sent to an external AI provider.

However, downloading models, installing dependencies, or using other online features may require an internet connection and communication with external services.

## 6. Model Downloads and External Resources

Okean.AI may allow you to download AI models and other resources from external sources.

For example, you may download GGUF models or use model-management features provided by supported AI engines.

Such downloads may connect to third-party websites or services. Those services may process technical information associated with your connection, such as your IP address.

Okean.AI does not control the privacy practices of external download providers.

## 7. Data Sharing

Okean.AI does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

The application does not operate an advertising or user-tracking system.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when required by features you choose to use, such as online AI APIs or external downloads.

## 8. Data Security and User Responsibility

Okean.AI is designed to give users control over their locally stored information.

However, no application or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for:
- Protecting access to your device.
- Keeping backups of important data.
- Managing your API keys securely.
- Reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services.
- Avoiding sharing sensitive information with external AI providers unless you understand their data practices.

## 9. Open-Source Transparency

Okean.AI is an open-source project.

Users can inspect the source code to understand how the application works and how it handles information.

We encourage transparency, community contributions, and independent verification.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Okean.AI evolves.

Any changes will be published in the project's official repository.

The latest version of this document will always be available there.

## 11. Contact

For questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please use the project's official GitHub repository and its available contact channels.
